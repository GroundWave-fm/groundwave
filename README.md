# GroundWave (`groundwave.fm`)

> **The Local-First Music & Creator Economy Platform**  
> High-fidelity music streaming, native artist & indie label storefronts, verified tastemaker curation, tiered fan memberships, and creator-governed micro-communities—anchored by geographic scene discovery.

---

## 📻 What is GroundWave?

Named after **ground wave radio propagation**—the physical wave that hugs the earth's topography to deliver authentic, high-quality local broadcasts—**GroundWave** solves the modern music platform trilemma:

1. **Hyperlocal Scene Discovery**: Ground discovery in local neighborhoods, metropolitan hubs, independent record labels, and live venue calendars instead of homogenized global algorithms.
2. **Integrated Streaming & Social Feed**: Continuous, lossless audio playback beneath a clean multimedia feed with zero-friction conversions (listen → buy vinyl → get concert tickets → subscribe).
3. **Independent Label Hubs**: Dedicated storefronts, multi-artist roster management, and "Vinyl of the Month" subscription clubs for indie labels.
4. **Verified Curator Broadcasting**: Tastemakers, radio DJs, and music journalists publish playlists, video essays, and podcasts using GroundWave music with automated track attribution and royalty splits.
5. **Artist & Label Governed Hubs (Anti-Spam)**: Fans interact and post *only* inside dedicated creator hubs with granular moderation, replacing fragmented Discord and Reddit channels.
6. **Full-Spectrum Monetization**: 92% direct payout on merch and subscriptions, user-centric streaming payouts, and 1-tap direct tipping with automated multi-party splits.
